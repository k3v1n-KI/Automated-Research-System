Two minutes. One promise.

Show, don't sell. The whole pitch is built around the search experience — because once they see how fast it is to find, verify, or ask, they're already convinced.

The pitch in one line

"Today, finding the right service for a patient takes twenty phone calls. With Pathways, it's one sentence. Let me show you."

30s Find 50s Verify 30s Ask 10s Close
00:00

Open with the friction.

10 seconds · no clicks yet
Say · before sharing screen

"If you're a navigator in Algoma right now, finding the right service for a patient means juggling three spreadsheets, an old PDF directory, and a group text. Every minute on the phone is a minute you're not with someone who needs you. Pathways replaces all of that with one search box. Watch."

01
Find — one sentence becomes ranked services.
The magic moment. Spend the most time here.
00:10 — 00:40
Say

"This is Rita. She just got a call from a school — 14-year-old, recent self-harm disclosure, no OHIP, family wants to avoid hospital. She has three minutes before her next call. Watch what she does."

"She doesn't pick filters. She doesn't know a category code. She just describes the case the way she'd say it out loud — and she can sketch it, dictate it, or tag it."

"Look — her sentence just became structured filters: MH, walk-in, uninsured, youth, Sault Ste. Marie. She didn't tag any of that. And here are ranked services across the entire region, in seconds."

Do
  • Open Pathways Hi-Fi.html
  • Click Dictate on the dark hero card. The sample sentence streams in (~4s) — let it finish.
  • Press ↵ Enter (or click Search) to fly to the results page.
  • Point at the auto-extracted chips above the results — that's the "she didn't tag any of that" moment.
  • Hover the top result so the audience tracks ranking, distance, and the green Verified badge.
02
Verify — is this information still true?
The trust answer. The reason they'll actually use it.
00:40 — 01:30
Say

"Every navigator asks the same question: how do I know this info is still good? Hours change. Programs close. Old directories lie."

"Look at the hours field. Verified by Mia at Group Health, six days ago. The intake number — verified by Tom this morning. Every field has a chain of custody — who, when, from where."

"Rita just called this clinic — they answered. She clicks Confirm. One second. That field is now fresh, and it just got promoted in search for the next navigator. That's it. That's the whole contribution."

Do
  • Click the top result to open the resource page.
  • Pause for two seconds. Let them read the green / amber / red badges next to each field.
  • Click Confirm on the Hours field. The toast appears. A history entry slides in below.
  • Scroll down so the chain-of-custody history feed is visible — every action, attributed.
  • Optional: open the Flag dialog on a different field, show the anonymous option, then Cancel.
03
Ask — when the index doesn't have it.
The honest answer. No system is complete; this one knows it.
01:30 — 02:00
Say

"Sometimes nothing fits. The kid needs a francophone counsellor with after-school hours. That's not in any directory, anywhere."

"Same search box. Post as Ask. Other navigators across Algoma see it. Three replies, including one with an actual resource attached. Next time someone searches for this case — it's in the index. Permanently."

Do
  • Navigate to Asks from the top nav (or open hifi/asks.html).
  • Land on Rita's open Ask at the top of the feed — three replies visible.
  • Point at the reply with the attached resource card. That's the knowledge-capture moment.
  • Don't resolve it. Leave it open. You're done.
02:00

Close — the one-sentence ask.

10 seconds · stop the demo, look up
Say · look them in the eye

"Find. Verify. Ask. One screen. No training. And every search makes the next one sharper. If we get Algoma navigators using this for thirty days, we'll have the best service map in Northern Ontario — built by the people who actually use it. Want to be one of the first ten?"

Pace

Stay on Verify (Beat 2) the longest — it's where skepticism lives and where you win it. Don't rush past Confirm.

Words to avoid

"Platform", "ecosystem", "AI-powered". Use "search box", "one click", "the next navigator". Real words for real work.

If they interrupt

Good — they're engaged. Answer briefly and steer back: "Great question. Let me finish the loop and that'll answer it."
