## Region Definitions

- **GTA:** Toronto, Etobicoke, North York, Scarborough, East York, York, Brampton, Mississauga, Markham, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Woodbridge, Oakville, Burlington, Milton, Georgetown, Ajax, Pickering, Whitby, Aurora, Bolton, Caledon, East Gwillimbury, Newmarket, Uxbridge, and nearby GTA municipalities.
- **Northern Ontario:** Sudbury, Sault Ste. Marie, Thunder Bay, North Bay, Kenora, Dryden, Cochrane, Timmins-area communities, and other northern municipalities.
- **Eastern Ontario:** Ottawa, Kingston, Cornwall, Belleville, Brockville, Pembroke, Peterborough, Hawkesbury, and surrounding eastern communities.
- **Southwestern Ontario:** Hamilton, London, Windsor, Kitchener-Waterloo, Guelph, Brantford, Cambridge, Sarnia-area, and surrounding southwestern communities.
- **Central Ontario:** Barrie, Orillia, Huntsville, Haliburton, Midland, Minden, Meaford, Collingwood, and surrounding central communities.
- External records are assigned to the nearest analytical Ontario region for the comparative geography table: Calgary and Lumsden are grouped with Northern Ontario, while Head of Jeddore is grouped with Eastern Ontario. These are proximity-based analytical assignments; the organizations are not being described as physically located in Ontario.

## Initial Interpretation

The benchmark is geographically concentrated in the GTA: 336 of 500 requested fields, or 67.2%, come from that region. This means the benchmark should not be described as geographically balanced. Northern, Eastern, Southwestern, and Central Ontario each contribute between 5.4% and 9.6% of the requested fields.

The unresolved cases are concentrated in the GTA: all 11 unresolved fields occur there. This is partly a volume effect because the GTA contributes two-thirds of the benchmark, so regional unresolved rates should also be reported. The unresolved rates are:

| Region | Unresolved rate |
|---|---:|
| GTA | 3.3% (11/336) |
| Northern Ontario | 0.0% (0/46) |
| Eastern Ontario | 0.0% (0/41) |
| Southwestern Ontario | 0.0% (0/46) |
| Central Ontario | 0.0% (0/27) |

The main geographic result is therefore not that rural records necessarily performed worse. On this benchmark, the non-GTA groups have no unresolved fields, but their sample sizes are small. The thesis should describe this as a descriptive benchmark pattern rather than evidence that Pathways performs better in rural areas.

The next analysis should add urbanicity separately from region. A regional label identifies where the organization is located; an urban/rural label describes the settlement context. These should not be conflated. For example, Sudbury and North Bay are regional urban centres within Northern Ontario, while smaller northern municipalities may be rural or remote.

## Caveats

- City labels were normalized for variants such as `Cornwall, ON`, `Sault Ste. Marie`, `St. Catharines`, and `Windsor, Ontario`.
- The initial draft placed seven records in an `Other / outside Ontario` group. Two Acton records and Oshawa were moved to the GTA, Kettle Point was moved to Southwestern Ontario, and the three external records were assigned to their nearest analytical Ontario regions as documented above.
- The outcome categories follow the revised manual-review rules: exact, supported non-exact, weakly supported, and unresolved.
- These are field-level counts. A single organization can contribute more than one requested field.
